Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing, quality assurance, and cross-border transport.

How does BaliTrade protect my payment?
Your payment is deposited directly to BaliTrade custody via Indonesian bank transfer or USDT. The seller only receives payment after the shipment arrives at your destination and you confirm receipt in satisfactory order.
What if the goods don't pass quality control?
If goods fail quality control, you choose: a full refund or a replacement.
Can I order more than the stock shown?
Yes. You can use 'Request a Product' or message the seller directly within the 3-participant chat to request custom quantities or bulk harvest production.
Which countries do you ship to?
We ship via air cargo from Bali to Russia & CIS destinations with export documents from Bali.
How long does shipping take?
Shipping time depends on destination — our team confirms it before you pay.
