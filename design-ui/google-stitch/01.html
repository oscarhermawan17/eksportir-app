Product Card Architecture Decisions

In cross-border B2B commerce between Indonesia and CIS, consumer metrics (like free returns) are replaced with B2B-critical anchors:

1. Price per Unit & Weight Tier

Shown in USD with prominent per-kg denominator for customs duty and freight calculations.

2. Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)

Prevents retail buyer churn; matches air cargo pallet packaging requirements (20kg / 50kg).

3. QC Inspection Assurance

Explicit tag reminds buyers that BaliTrade agents open and test boxes prior to air dispatch.

4. Geographic Origin

Seller locality (e.g. Bangli, Ubud) anchors authentic Balinese origin.

Trilingual Architecture Principles

BaliTrade connects Balinese village artisans and coffee cooperatives (who communicate primarily in Bahasa Indonesia) with importers, roasteries, and boutique hotel buyers across Russia, Kazakhstan, and Uzbekistan (who communicate in Russian).

Dual-Language Legal Contracts

All generated commercial invoices and Air Waybills carry English + Russian / Indonesian terms side-by-side.

Preserve Original Terminology

Indonesian terms like "Arabika Kintamani", "Single Origin", "GrainPro", and "Giling Basah" are retained with tooltips.
