Grown on the volcanic slopes of Mount Batur in Kintamani, Bangli Regency, at an altitude of 1,200 to 1,500 meters above sea level. This high elevation combined with rich volcanic soil and shade-grown cultivation beneath citrus and avocado trees imparts a distinctive, bright citrus acidity and smooth floral aroma characteristic of genuine Balinese Arabica.

The lot undergoes traditional wet hulling (washed process), followed by patio sun-drying and meticulous hand-sorting by the local cooperative. Harvested in early 2026, the green coffee beans maintain an optimal moisture reading between 11% and 12%, meeting strict Grade 1 export standards for specialty roasters across Russia and CIS countries.
