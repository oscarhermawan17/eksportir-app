About the Workshop

Auto-translated from Indonesian · View original

Ubud Weavers brings together traditional artisan weaving families based in Gianyar Regency, Bali. For generations, our artisans have crafted functional homeware and accessories from sustainably harvested local plant fibers, including wild ata grass from Karangasem foothills and seasoned natural rattan. Each piece is hand-split, intricately woven, and naturally sun-dried and smoke-cured over coconut husks to give it its distinctive golden hue and natural moisture resistance.

Through BaliTrade, our collective works directly with overseas buyers in Russia and CIS countries. Every batch of ready stock is stored in our Gianyar studio and inspected by the BaliTrade Denpasar QC team before consolidated air shipment.
