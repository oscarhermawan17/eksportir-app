Product Card Architecture Decisions

In cross-border B2B commerce between Indonesia and CIS, consumer metrics are replaced with B2B-critical anchors:

1. Price per Unit & Weight Tier

Shown in USD with prominent per-kg denominator for freight calculations and customs valuation.

2. Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)

Prevents retail buyer churn; matches air cargo pallet packaging requirements (20kg / 50kg).

3. QC Inspection Assurance

Explicit tag reminds buyers that BaliTrade agents open and test boxes prior to air dispatch.

4. Geographic Origin & Verified Producer

Seller locality (e.g. Bangli, Ubud) anchors authentic Balinese origin with matching ratings (38 reviews).

Trilingual Architecture Principles

BaliTrade connects Balinese village artisans and coffee cooperatives with importers, roasteries, and boutique hotel buyers across Russia, Kazakhstan, and Uzbekistan. Clean language switching keeps navigation intuitive while standardizing negotiations in English.

Standard Text Codes (EN / ID / RU)

Clean textual ISO indicators replace national flag icons for neutrality and clarity across regional borders.

Preserve Original Terminology

Indonesian terms like "Arabika Kintamani", "Single Origin", "GrainPro", and "Giling Basah" are retained across all language modes.
